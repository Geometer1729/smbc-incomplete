Incomplete

A game of hidden motives, from SMBC

How to play

Incomplete is Tic-Tac-Toe with a twist: you don't necessarily want to win.

Each game, you and the AI are secretly assigned a preference ordering over the three possible outcomes — X wins, Draw, and O wins. Your ordering is shown to you; the AI's is hidden.

For example, your objective might be Draw > O wins > X wins — meaning a draw is your best outcome, the other player winning is middle, and you actually winning is your worst outcome.

You play as X and always go first. The AI plays using a Nash equilibrium strategy, so good luck reading its intentions.
